How it works

One connected system. A clearer picture of your home.

GridSense brings together household monitoring, room sensing and connected plug-in smart switches to help people manage unnecessary energy use.

1

Monitor household electricity

A central monitoring unit is installed at the home's main electrical input to track overall electricity use.

2

Understand room activity

Room sensors use radar-based detection to identify occupancy and activity without cameras.

3

Manage connected appliances

Plug-in smart switches connect compatible appliances to the GridSense system for monitoring and control.

4

Make informed decisions

The app helps users view energy information, manage connected devices and see estimated savings.

Good questions

GridSense, explained.

A few quick answers about the idea behind the system.

Does GridSense use cameras?

No. The proposed room sensors use radar-based detection to identify room occupancy and activity. They are designed to avoid camera-based monitoring.

Do I need to replace my wall outlets?

No. GridSense smart switches are designed as plug-in adapters that fit into ordinary wall outlets. Compatible appliances plug into the adapter.

Will GridSense automatically lower my electricity bill?

GridSense is designed to help households identify and manage unnecessary electricity use. Actual savings are not guaranteed and will depend on how the household uses the system.

Can I control my home when I'm away?

The proposed app includes remote monitoring and control features for compatible connected appliances, subject to the system's final design and connectivity.

How is GridSense installed?

The proposed system includes a central monitoring unit installed by a qualified technician, along with room sensors, control panels and plug-in smart switches.
